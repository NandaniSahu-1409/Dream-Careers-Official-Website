WHICH TO CHOOSE?

B.Pharm vs D.Pharm — Quick Comparison

B.Pharmacy (4 Years)

  • Full degree — more career options
  • Eligible for Drug Inspector, R&D roles
  • Can pursue M.Pharm, MBA, Pharm.D
  • Higher salary potential (₹3–15 LPA)
  • Eligible for government gazetted posts
  • Can open own pharmacy + clinical roles
  • Best for long-term career growth

D.Pharmacy (2 Years)

  • Faster entry into pharmacy career
  • Own medical store / retail pharmacy
  • Hospital pharmacist, dispenser roles
  • Lower investment, quicker returns
  • Can upgrade to B.Pharm later
  • Good for entrepreneurship (own shop)
  • Salary range ₹1.5–4 LPA to start
