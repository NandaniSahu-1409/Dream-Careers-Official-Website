MCC Process

MCC 4-Round Online Counselling Structure

Pure algorithm-based counselling - NEET rank + choice order + seat availability

1 Round 1

Registration + fee payment

Choice filling and choice locking

Seat allotment

Reporting to the college

Free exit is allowed (you can leave without losing security deposit)

2 Round 2

No free exit now

If allotted and do not join or resign later, security deposit is forfeited

For Deemed universities, security deposit can be ₹2 lakh

3 Round 3 (Mop-up)

Often called the Mop-up round

Fresh registration allowed for candidates who didn't join earlier

New choice filling based on leftover seats

4 Round 4 (Mop-up)

Last round with system-generated allotments

Candidates cannot change choices

Only for remaining vacant seats

Important:

There is no interview and no "influence" in MCC counselling. It is a pure algorithm based on NEET rank + your choice order + seat availability.
